# Bounded outcome validation — 29 October 2026

The user requested stronger evidence without response-by-response human review. This targeted validation has 24 explicit cases, three repetitions for each of eight scenarios: five second-displayed-option selection scenarios across wedding, birthday, baby shower, corporate and social events (15 conversations); one- and two-provider simulated quote completion with exact IDs, event date and no-repeat receipt assertions (six); and a synthetic provider detail-value scenario (three). Exact cases and order are in `selection.json`. Results are a new separate targeted population and do not replace or pool with previous scores.

For selection, the hard `provider_displayed_selection` assertion checks a unique numbered option in an origin-verified first response against a uniquely matched same-turn provider ID and the recorded selected ID on the later origin-verified turn. Missing or ambiguous evidence is fail-closed and explicitly identified as unassessable; it is not treated as factual failure. Cases require at least two candidates, but candidate count does not guarantee the reply presented two identifiable options. Public provider reads are live only for the selection cases; private customer access and all SE mutations are mocked. Quote and detail worlds are controlled fixtures. Quote receipt checks do not prove external delivery. Literal detail checks do not establish full semantic accuracy or currency.

The planned scenario population is deliberate, not a customer sample. Report outcomes separately by family and per case; do not infer overall service success, user satisfaction, naturalness, complete grounding or business impact. No semantic judge or human labeling is used. Keep failures, source/fixture/deployment hashes, all run artifacts, and before/after identity. The estimated spend depends on actual observed tokens and dated rates; it is not an invoice. The user authorized an approximate $5 budget for the earlier study work; retained prior model/Lambda estimates total under $1 before this run, leaving substantial headroom. No automatic retries or unselected live gate runs are authorized.
