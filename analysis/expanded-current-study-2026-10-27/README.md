# Expanded current-artifact thesis study

This separately frozen study extends the current master into one contiguous run: 84 distinct scenarios repeated three times, 252 scheduled conversations. It retains 34 non-OTP scenarios from the current follow-up, replaces the calculator case with the separately validated source-published URL case, and adds 50 cases derived from July V4's underlying V3 scenario texts. The July V4 overlay only changed an internal terminal-path rule for one scenario; the new study does not reuse that historical internal-node gate. Each of five July event groups has all ten historical route labels and three planned repetitions. Inputs and source-to-new-case mapping are retained in `july-scenario-mapping.json`.

Private customer access and every SE effect are mocked. Forty-five July-derived scenarios use live public provider reads, while the five no-results scenarios use an explicitly empty fixture. Thus those five validate behavior in a controlled empty world, not the actual marketplace's absence of a match. Other current-master cases keep their recorded fixture/read mode. Real provider catalog and index changes remain a source of variability.

The primary metric is conversations satisfying all prespecified hard assertions / 252 scheduled conversations; errors and missing executions remain in the denominator. Report the existing three strata and five new event-group strata, each July route family, failures and errors, and scenario-cluster bootstrap uncertainty. Do not pool the results with the earlier October or July studies or present the score difference as a causal improvement. OTP scenarios are absent by user direction before this protocol was frozen; their errors remain visible in the previous study.

The new July-derived hard assertions check the extracted event and requested need categories, a delivered response, zero unrequested quote/RSVP/handoff effects, and selected additional observable obligations: candidate/link presence for recommendation and multi-need cases; no invented location for clarification; a recorded selected status for selection; and no shortlist in the empty fixture. Route labels are task descriptions, not proof that each route's complete semantics passed. In particular, the first displayed provider's identity is not independently checked in selection, no-results text is not semantically judged, FAQ meaning and refinement constraints are not comprehensively checked, and recovery-labeled cases do not inject a transport failure. The closure prompts do not provide an event date, so their check is that no quote is submitted without one; they do not test successful quote delivery. Correct URLs do not establish all provider attributes or complete response faithfulness.

Inputs, fixture and source hashes, ordered seeded schedule, deployment identity and hard assertions are frozen before any paid model request. The pre-declared budget stops new admissions at $4 estimated, retaining margin for the earlier estimated study spend under the user's approximately $5 budget. Charges are estimated from reported token and modeled Lambda usage; no claim of actual invoiced spend is made. All executed outcomes and failure artifacts are retained; no semantic retry or mid-run tuning occurs. The engine verifies the development artifact and configuration before and after execution. Median/p95 latency and instruction/token telemetry are descriptive. The fixed-seed scenario-cluster bootstrap interval captures sensitivity to the declared scenario set, not real-customer sampling uncertainty. No inference about satisfaction, naturalness, business impact or causal improvement is intended.
