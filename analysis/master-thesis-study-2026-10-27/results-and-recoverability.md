Run `eval-2026-10-27T05-31-20-436Z-edb558f3`, 27 October 2026. This is a new population, separate from July V4 and later targeted validations. The prespecified study consists of 30 scenarios, each repeated three times: 90 conversations and 174 turns. Eight provider scenarios used live public reads and two controlled provider fixtures; customer data and effects were mocked. FAQ cases used the retained development knowledge base. ## Prespecified results

70 of 90 conversations passed every hard obligation; 20 failed, with no execution errors or skipped conversations. Provider scenarios passed 28/30 (live public reads 22/24, controlled 6/6); FAQ/customer service 23/30; continuity, authentication and effects 19/30. Twenty-one scenarios passed all three repetitions, six were mixed and three failed all three. The fixed-seed stratified scenario-cluster bootstrap gives a 95% interval of 65.6%–88.9% for the conversation pass rate of 77.8%. This interval describes uncertainty under resampling of these designed scenarios, not representativeness of real users. Details and denominators are in `master-summary.json` and `master-conversations.json`.

Estimated cost was $0.16751: $0.141469 in model usage and $0.026041 modeled Lambda cost, using the harness's dated rates and observed usage. This is not an invoice or actual-cost API measurement. The model generated 64 detectable provider URL spans, all matched to a same-turn provider trace; this bounded check does not evaluate other statements or comprehensive factual faithfulness.

## Inspection of the 20 failed conversations

* **Support contact, 3/3:** diagnostic output redacted the expected WhatsApp URL, so the text assertion failed. The retained original model responses were retrieved without new generation; reconstructed delivered text matches the recorded SHA-256 in all three, and all three contain the expected URL. These are demonstrably recoverable measurement failures. The original score remains unchanged; see `artifacts/faq-hash-recovery.json`.
* **Calculator, 2/3:** the same redaction prevented the check from observing the delivered link. Hash-verified recovery shows an English path, `/cost-of-service`, rather than the exact expected Spanish path. The English path is present in the retained knowledge-base article, but its existence in that source does not establish that the destination works or that it meets the prespecified exact-URL assertion. These remain failures under the original oracle.
* **Authentication refusal, 3/3, and RSVP durability, 2/3:** hard effect assertions passed, but the output-origin gate rejected suppressed turns because it expects classifier evidence while the trace records a router suppression decision. The evidence contract can be repaired, but suppression itself may be inappropriate for substantive privacy refusal or a repeated attendance decision. The current evidence does not justify marking these cases as passes automatically.
* **Number-word OTP, 3/3:** downstream authentication/handoff effects and missing origin evidence contribute to failures. The fixture and authenticated customer-event lookup may also be incomplete; the evidence does not isolate a single cause. Two repetitions include objective unexpected mocked effects, so they cannot be dismissed as redaction alone.
* **Gift mixed order, 2/3:** an unexpected mocked handoff and missing purchase evidence occurred on a follow-up; one repetition passed. This is a meaningful functional failure needing further investigation.
* **Live multi-need provider request, 2/3:** search did not run and the response asked for clarification. This was not an observed catalog outage. It may reflect either a behavior issue or disagreement between the immediate-search assertion and clarification policy; preserve the failure pending a defined contract.
* **Provider completion, 2/3:** the expected finish and quote tools were not called; responses did not claim a false receipt. The fixture's requested event date was already past at run time, and its provider detail data is sparse. These are plausible confounders, not a proven root cause. A future fixture should use a valid date and complete details.
* **Question after takeover, 1/3:** the later response was blocked by the `support_lane_unverified_handoff_claim` delivery guard; the requested date was not delivered. This is an observable response failure, while the cause needs further investigation.

The categories above sum to 20 failed conversations. A corrected diagnostic view can be computed from retained evidence; it must be explicitly labeled post hoc and must not replace the original results. Changes to routing, fixture contracts, or assertions should be evaluated in a separately identified future study, without retrying or silently revising this run.
