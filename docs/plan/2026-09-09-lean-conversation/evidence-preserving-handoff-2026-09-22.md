## Architecture decision: extractor as proposal, not information gate

Keep a single structured extractor. It reports `task`, `subject/referents`, `requested facets`, `source certainty`, `ambiguity`, and explicitly requested mutation/action. Avoid requiring it to choose the one true backend partition or precompose the answer. The current `prompts/extractors/information.txt` mixes source ownership with aspect examples and says `payment_details` is only for payment time. Replace this with one ownership contract: known order/cart -> orders; known gift purchase -> gift purchases; unestablished source -> bounded purchase discovery. Amount, payment, delivery and shipping are facets independent of source. “How much remains?” requires total, paid and remaining-verifiability facts, not a computed due amount. “Did hosts receive it?” requires current payment/posting evidence and gift-credit mechanism when present.

The runtime validates extraction against current typed plan and source coverage. Missing or uncertain source cannot become `orders-empty` or `no account purchase` after reading one partition. A descriptive event/item hint may rank or identify a read answer in the model's reasoning but cannot erase candidates. A strong explicit ID mismatch blocks mutation and prevents opportunistic selection. For support follow-ups, actual delivered history and the established issue outrank a later extractor summary saying “no problem specified.” The model may decide a supplied detail changes the answer, but a detail-only turn must not reset the issue.

The canonical profile contains all authorized customer orders, gifts, invitations, item amounts/types, dates, status and provenance for that customer, without age truncation or duplicate record copies. Per-turn projection selects the relevant *domain* and safe fields; it does not drop record facts needed to answer the user's current question. Do not send unrelated planning/provider catalogs on support turns. Sensitive identifiers remain authorization-gated. Preserve explicit source coverage and field availability so `null` never ambiguously means withheld, not read, and absent at source.
