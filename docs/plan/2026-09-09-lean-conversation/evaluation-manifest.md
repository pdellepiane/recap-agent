4. All runs retained with run IDs: every passing and failing run, including superseded attempts; never delete or rescore a run.
5. Before/after real-request metrics at the transport boundary (E10 capture; identical denominators over matched scenarios).
6. Removed-path inventory (old caller removed plus new production caller exercised per E01-E12 edit).
7. Output-origin comparisons (sentinel survival to delivery per R01).
8. Mutation results (mutant ID, intended failing assertion, observed failure per R08).
9. Holdout limits (robustness-only label, seed record, independence limits per R10 section above).

Missing evidence is failed/incomplete acceptance, never "not applicable" unless that exact exclusion was predeclared for the scenario. No score, prose summary, or screenshot substitutes for these artifacts. Owner/reviewer verifies the final diff and manifest.
