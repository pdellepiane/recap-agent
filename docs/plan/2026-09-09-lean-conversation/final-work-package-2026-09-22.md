## Architecture decision

Keep one structured extractor, the existing bounded information executor, one canonical evidence projection and one model-written reply. Runtime validates authorized scope, exact mutation target, invariant facts and verified effect receipts. It does not choose customer-facing words or infer conversational intent from keywords. Authorized customer profile records remain complete, including older purchases, item amounts, invitation state and provenance. No date filter, fixed reply, extra answer model, global rules dump, new state machine, or source guessed from an aspect such as shipping. A receipt is image evidence, not backend approval. Unknown paid means unknown remaining, never full due.

The response classifier is currently a third model call and added roughly 1.3–1.6 seconds in sampled turns. Do not remove it during this release correction: merging delivery disposition into extraction changes silence/automation behavior and needs its own validation. Record a separate post-release simplification task to fold its typed decision into extraction without extra calls or canned responses.
